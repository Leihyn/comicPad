The Problem

Comic Creators Face Major Challenges:

  • 💸 High Platform Fees: Traditional platforms take 30-50% of creator revenue
  • 🔒 Ownership Issues: Creators don't truly own their digital comics
  • 🌐 Limited Global Reach: Geographic restrictions on distribution
  • 📊 Opaque Analytics: No transparent sales tracking

The Solution - Comic Pad

A Decentralized NFT Marketplace for Comic Creators

  • 🎨 Create & Mint: Upload comics, mint NFT collections on Hedera (HTS), IPFS storage
  • 💰 Instant Monetization: List NFTs, direct creator-to-fan sales, low fees (~$0.0001)
  • 🌍 Global Access: Borderless marketplace, crypto payments, transparent ownership
  • 📈 Creator Control: Set prices, royalties on resales, full content ownership

How It Works

For Creators:

  • Connect Wallet → HashPack integration
  • Create Collection → Mint NFT collection on Hedera
  • Upload Comic → Episodes with cover + pages to IPFS
  • Mint NFTs → Limited edition digital comics
  • List on Marketplace → Set price, start selling

For Collectors:

  • Browse Marketplace → Discover comics
  • Buy NFT → Instant ownership transfer
  • Read Online → Built-in comic reader
  • Resell → Secondary market with creator royalties
